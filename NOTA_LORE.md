- **The 90/10 Split:** 90% of the UI is the analytical scaffolding (`IBM Plex Sans`). 10% is the wet-ink emotional soul (`Instrument Serif Italic` with `-0.01em` tracking). If everything is poetry, nothing is.

## 2. The Biological Dot
The Dot is the absolute biological core of the interface. It possesses four strict states:
1. **Idle:** Static, resting.
2. **Save:** Fills with physical ink over `200ms` to settle.
3. **Active:** A subtle, continuous breathing pulse.
4. **Alignment:** Emits a slight glow (`--olive` #6B7250) when resonance is achieved.

## 3. Symphonic Haptic Architecture
Standard device vibrations are cheap. We dictate rhythm and tempo.
- **Light Selection:** A crisp 30ms pulse.
- **Trace Left (Memory Seal):** A heavy, resonant 200ms thud.
- **Acoustic Contract:** Silence is default. Any UI sounds must be analog (heavy paper rustle, glass clink) and capped at a strict, low volume threshold.

## 4. Situational Intent (The "Dark Side of Delight" Rule)
Delight must never compete with usability. The UI must recognize time-of-day urgency.
- **Morning Ritual Mode:** Suppress the 1200ms ceremonial holds. Strip away poetic friction. Shift to instant, functional utility.
- **Evening Desk Mode:** Introduce warmer amber/charcoal biases, slower transitions, and reduced simultaneous choices.

## 5. Tactile Physics & Anti-Perfect UI
- **Structural vs. Organic:** Structural containers get `0px` border-radius (cut paper/glass). Organic elements (pulse points, the Dot) get full rounding.
- **Bendy UI:** When a curator presses a structural card, employ "bendy" physics where the interface physically reacts and bends at the exact point of the thumb.
- **Analog Dissonance:** Break perfect digital symmetry. Rotate stamps or botanical sketches by `-1.5 degrees`. Use `mix-blend-multiply` so ink physically sinks into the `--ivory` linen grain.
