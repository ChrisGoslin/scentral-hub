# nota. — Brand Identity & Voice

## Name & Dot Doctrine

- **Product name:** nota. (lowercase, always with the dot)
- **The dot is the signal:** not punctuation, not decoration — a mark of recognition, presence, and memory
- **Never modified:** no "nota.io", "Nota", "NOTA" (all wrong)
- **When read aloud:** "nota dot" or just "nota"
- **When written alongside other text:** maintain the lowercase and dot (`find your identity at nota.`)

---

## Tone & Character

**Core voice:** calm, intelligent, observational, slightly playful, restrained.

### Allowed tones
- **Calm:** matter-of-fact, unhurried, inviting reflection
- **Observational:** noticing patterns, seeing clearly, naming what's already there
- **Slightly playful:** dry humour, understated wit, a knowing smile
- **Restrained:** never loud, never oversold, never breathless
- **Warm:** human, considerate, unpretentious

### Banned tones
- Loud / hyped (no "SHOP NOW", no exclamation abundance)
- Gimmicky (no "join the scent revolution", no AI-startup language)
- Salesy (no "don't miss out", no artificial urgency)
- Influencer-led (no "as seen on…", no aspirational lifestyles, no luxury clichés)
- Luxury marketing (no "exclusive", "prestigious", "prestige", "indulgent", "curated for you")
- Flowery / overstated (no "enchanting", "magical", "transformative" applied to commerce)
- Generic AI (no "powered by AI", no machine-learning hype)

---

## Language System

### Emotional / Identity Layer
Use second-person observational language:
- "You tend to…" (pattern recognition, non-judgmental)
- "This fits you" (validation, quiet confidence)
- "Still fits" (continuity, evolution acknowledged)
- "On the nose" (accurate, spot-on, understood)
- "Something's shifted" (change detected, not blamed)
- "Noted." (acknowledgement, memory, finality)

### Feature-Level Copy
- **The Read / Noseprint:** "Your identity" (not "your profile", not "your scent archetype")
- **Shelf:** "My Shelf" (ownership, collection)
- **Traces:** "What does this smell like? Don't explain it. Describe it." (instruction, not question)
- **Trails:** "Guided understanding paths" (not "threads", "forums", "communities")
- **Blind Ranking:** "Remove bias" (not "test objectively", not "blind test")
- **Evolution:** "Something's shifted." (pattern detected, not "update your identity")

### Avoided Patterns
- No "scent genome", "olfactory DNA", "scent profile"
- No "match", "recommendation" (use "match", "pairing", "fit" — avoid algorithmic framing)
- No metric-first language ("87% compatibility") — names and feelings come first
- No "join the community", "help us build", "contribute your taste"
- No "unlock", "discover the secret", "explore" (too app-store)

## Copy Examples

### ✅ Good (nota. voice)
- "Come back to your shelf." (sign-in prompt)
- "You consistently describe scent through places." (Insights intro)
- "This might sit differently on your skin. Try it again in cooler air." (Aura advisory)
- "You were solar, minimalist. Leaning toward dark, alchemist." (Evolution card)
- "Oh no. This again." (Temptation trigger — dry humour)
- "Room to be wrong." (Empty shelf slot)

### ❌ Wrong (don't use)
- "Unlock your scent potential" (gimmicky)
- "AI-powered fragrance discovery" (hype)
- "Your personalized scent genome" (pseudoscience)
- "Join the fragrance revolution" (loud, influencer)
- "Curated just for you" (luxury cliché, false intimacy)
- "Discover Your True Scent" (salesy)

## Design Language

### Colours
- **Primary palette:** muted, soft-contrast (no pure black/white)
- **Accent:** gold/amber (`--color-accent`)
- **Backgrounds:** parchment, slate, charcoal
- **No gradients, gloss, or shine**
- **No neon or high-saturation colours**

### Typography
- **Primary:** Unbounded (humanist sans, 90% of surfaces)
- **Editorial:** Cormorant Garamond italic (10%, Noseprint reveals, major moments)
- **Never:** decorative fonts, mixed display fonts, script

### Motion
**Verbs:** reveal, drift, morph, fade, settle, breathe  
**Timing:** `--motion-fast` 150–250ms | `--motion-medium` 300–500ms | `--motion-slow` 1000–2000ms  
**Banned:** bounce, overshoot, wiggle, flip, shake

### Spacing & Density
- **Generous margins** (breathing room)
- **Tight text blocks** (80–100 char lines when possible)
- **Calm density** (no infinite scroll, no like-count dopamine loops)

## 2036 Test

**Before shipping anything:** would this feel dated or trendy in 10 years?

- ✅ Honest, observational language stays true
- ✅ Functional, purposeful design survives
- ❌ Hype language dates immediately
- ❌ Trend-following aesthetics age poorly
- ❌ Gimmicks rot fast

---

## When in Doubt

**Ask:** "Is this calm, observational, and restrained? Does it respect the user's intelligence? Is it the simplest true thing to say?"

If the answer is no, rewrite.
