## Expert Layering Protocols

### Protocol: Protocol Alpha: Iris-Patchouli Absolute
- **Concept:** Bridges powdery floral domain with dark, boozy oriental structure. Marries the waxy cosmetic iris of Kayaan Classic with the benzoin-patchouli base of Rochas Moustache EDP for a hyper-elegant formal signature.
- **Components:** Al Wataniah Kayaan Classic (2 sprays) + Rochas Moustache EDP (2 sprays)
- **Expected Performance:** Sillage: Smooth, spiced cacao-patchouli aura, Longevity: 10+ hours hours
- **Occasion:** Formal, Date Night (Cold/Universal)
- **Note:** Apply Kayaan Classic to pulse points first. Let settle 10 min. Apply Moustache EDP over the same zones.

### Protocol: Protocol Beta: Raspberry Smoke Engine
- **Concept:** Simulates a burning, opulent Middle Eastern lounge. High-contrast, high-density matrix of raspberry/oud over dry peppery suede.
- **Components:** Paris Corner Rifaaqat (1 sprays) + French Avenue Nomad (1 sprays)
- **Expected Performance:** Sillage: Inescapable raspberry-oud-suede beast mode aura, Longevity: 16+ hours hours
- **Occasion:** Evening, Statement Events (Cold)
- **Note:** Do NOT apply to identical dermal zones. Rifaaqat on chest, Nomad on wrists. Or both on back of neck only.

### Protocol: Protocol Gamma: Billionaire's Rum & Vanilla
- **Concept:** Ultimate boozy winter gourmand. The sharp leather of Bentley cuts the extreme sweetness of Liquid Brun while vanilla smooths the Bentley's chaotic opening.
- **Components:** Bentley For Men Intense (1 sprays) + French Avenue Liquid Brun (2 sprays)
- **Expected Performance:** Sillage: Shifts between aged rum and sweet baked pastries depending on airflow, Longevity: 12+ hours hours
- **Occasion:** Date Night, Winter Evenings (Cold)
- **Note:** Apply Bentley as single foundational spray to chest. Follow with 2 sprays Liquid Brun over the top on same zone.

### Protocol: Protocol Delta: Chypre-Akigalawood Vibration
- **Concept:** Aggressively fresh modern masculine for high-heat or high-kinetic environments. Superimpose heavy oakmoss/blackcurrant of SNOI over vibrating metallic spice of Vulcan Feu.
- **Components:** French Avenue Vulcan Feu (2 sprays) + Afnan S. Not Only Intense (2 sprays)
- **Expected Performance:** Sillage: Inescapable sparkling, tart, deeply woody aura, Longevity: 14+ hours hours
- **Occasion:** Clubbing, Outdoor Events, High Heat (Warm/Universal)
- **Note:** Apply Vulcan Feu to chest/shoulders. Wait 5 min. Apply SNOI to hair and upper back. Never front of neck.

### Protocol: The Gourmand Alchemist
- **Concept:** Amplifies the rich, boozy-vanilla heart of French Avenue Royal Blend by cutting through its heavy texture with the crisp, metallic iris and sophisticated lavender of Kayaan Classic.
- **Components:** French Avenue Royal Blend (2 sprays) + Kayaan Classic (3 sprays)
- **Expected Performance:** Sillage: Strong, Longevity: 9 hours
- **Occasion:** Date Night / Formal Evening (Autumn / Winter)
- **Note:** Apply Royal Blend first to the pulse points of the neck. Wait 90 seconds for the initial alcohol flash-off to settle before misting Kayaan Classic directly over it to let the powdery iris bind with the warm cognac base.

### Protocol: Smoked Tobacco & Vintage Woods
- **Concept:** Supercharges the cozy, boozy pub aesthetic of Jazz Club by pairing it with the retro, plummy plum-wood and tea-like elegance of Vintage Radio.
- **Components:** Jazz Club (3 sprays) + Vintage Radio (3 sprays)
- **Expected Performance:** Sillage: Moderate-to-Strong, Longevity: 8 hours
- **Occasion:** Lounge / Night Out (Autumn / Winter)
- **Note:** Spray Jazz Club on the chest and wrists. Immediately layer Vintage Radio on top. The woody-gourmand molecules of Palo Santo and Plum in Vintage Radio will instantly tame the sharp nicotine-tobacco edges of Jazz Club.

### Protocol: Incandescent Frost
- **Concept:** Contrasts the deep, dark, multi-faceted spice of Lattafa Fire On Ice with the hyper-vibrant, icy blue-mint freshness of Rasasi Hawas Ice to create a magnetic, hot-and-cold aura.
- **Components:** Lattafa Fire On Ice (2 sprays) + Rasasi Hawas Ice (3 sprays)
- **Expected Performance:** Sillage: Beast Mode, Longevity: 11 hours
- **Occasion:** Nightclubs / High-Energy Events (Spring / Summer / Transition Weather)
- **Note:** WARNING: High concentrations of Ambroxan in both fragrances can cause immediate olfactory fatigue. Spray Lattafa Fire On Ice exclusively on the back of the neck. Spray Hawas Ice on the shoulders to keep the sillage trail dynamic without overwhelming your own nose.

### Protocol: Gourmand Fougère Fusion
- **Concept:** Injects the ultra-creamy, lactonic sweetness of Lattafa Eclaire with a sophisticated, aromatic backbone using Rochas Moustache EDP's refined mandarin, rose, and patchouli profile.
- **Components:** Rochas Moustache EDP (3 sprays) + Lattafa Eclaire (2 sprays)
- **Expected Performance:** Sillage: Strong, Longevity: 10 hours
- **Occasion:** Evening Casual / Cozy Dates (Winter / Spring)
- **Note:** Apply the aromatic base of Rochas Moustache EDP to skin first. Let it dry completely for 3 minutes before layering Lattafa Eclaire over it. This allows the heavy vanilla/caramel molecules to trap the volatile citrus and rose top notes of Moustache, extending their life.
