**Full Description (4000 characters max, British English):**

nota. is a fragrance app built for people who actually care about what they smell like. Not encyclopaedic collectors — just people with a growing wardrobe and a nose for what works.

✦ DISCOVER 280+ FRAGRANCES
Find dupes, Middle Eastern gems, designer alternatives, and hidden niche brands. Written for beginners, not enthusiasts. Search by brand, vibe, or plain-English descriptions.

✦ BUILD YOUR WARDROBE IN SECONDS
Add bottles to your collection. Rate them by how often you actually reach for them. Watch your shelf organise itself by affinity — signatures at the top, experiments at the bottom.

✦ FIND YOUR SCENT IDENTITY
The 15-second Sanctuary Profiler asks two questions and returns a persona: The Velvet Intellectual, The Solar Minimalist, or The Dark Alchemist. Your identity shapes what you see in the app.

✦ LAYER LIKE A PRO
The Layering Lab suggests what works together and explains why in plain English, not note pyramids. Get pairing suggestions backed by olfactory logic.

✦ KNOW YOUR SHELF
The Living Wardrobe organises your collection into four tiers: Top Signatures (daily drivers), Occasion Modifiers (special moments), Base Anchors (versatile staples), and Holding Zone (still testing). Drag, drop, sort by house or season.

✦ DISCOVER CHEAPER ALTERNATIVES
See what each fragrance was inspired by. Compare the copy to the original. Stop paying £150 for £18.

✦ WEAR STREAKS
Log what you wore today. Build your streak. Unlock your scent confidence.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED.
No reviews you didn't ask for. No algorithm playing favourites. Just a better way to know your collection.

PRIVACY-FIRST.
Your collection is anonymous and stored locally on your device. We don't store your email.
