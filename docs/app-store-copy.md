# Scentral — App Store Marketing Copy

## Localized Marketing (British English)

### App Name
Scentral — Your Fragrance Wardrobe

### Subtitle
Scent layering & clone maps.

### Description
Find your signature scent without the luxury markup. Scentral is your personal fragrance intelligence app, designed to help you catalogue your collection, discover premium inspired-by alternatives, and master the science of scent layering.

**SCENT IDENTITY**
Take the Scentral Sanctuary profiler to reveal your scent persona. Whether you’re a Velvet Intellectual or a Dark Alchemist, we’ll help you find the fragrances that speak your language.

**THE CLONE MAP**
Discover Middle Eastern houses (Lattafa, Afnan, Armaf) that punch above their weight. We map the olfactory DNA of the world’s most coveted scents to reveal accessible, high-performance alternatives.

**THE LAYERING LAB**
Experiment with your collection. Use our AURA engine to build custom fragrance combinations tailored to your environment, social energy, and desired projection.

**YOUR DIGITAL WARDROBE**
Organise your bottles on virtual walnut shelves. Track your daily wear, manage maceration countdowns, and build a scent trail that lasts.
