## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Schedule everything by T-3.** Don't wing it on launch day—you'll be too busy monitoring PH and Reddit.

2. **Monitor the first 6 hours live.** PH's algorithm ranks hard on day 1 velocity. Early engagement matters. Stay alert T+0 12:01 AM to 6:00 AM.

3. **Respond to EVERY comment for the first 24 hours.** Speed = signal to the algorithm. Also builds community goodwill.

4. **Screenshot your metrics daily.** By T+7 you'll have data that's gold for follow-up emails, tweets, and a founder story (if you want to write one).

5. **Don't add features on launch week.** Focus is stability + engagement. Feature work starts T+8.

6. **If something breaks, post immediately.** Transparency builds trust. "Experiencing issues, working on it, updates in 15 min" beats radio silence.

7. **Engage with fragrance threads even outside your launch posts.** Be helpful. Answer questions. Show you know your domain. People will organically come to your posts.

## 🔄 Iteration Notes

**If metrics miss targets:**

- **Downloads < 500:** Too small reach on social. Increase Reddit presence, reach out to fragrance influencers.
- **PH ranking < Top 20:** Boost engagement (respond faster, share more data, post update comments with metrics).
- **Low persona completion (<40%):** Onboarding questions too confusing. Test shorter version (1 question → automatic persona).
- **Low bottle-add rate (<25%):** Wardrobe not compelling. Check which persona + bottles are underperforming. Adjust persona descriptions or top bottles.

Don't iterate during launch week. Collect feedback, make notes, iterate T+8 onward.
