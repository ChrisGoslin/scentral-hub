# Scentral Launch — 15-Second Demo Video Script

**Format:** Vertical video (9:16, TikTok/Instagram Reels format)  
**Platform:** Twitter, Instagram, Product Hunt, TikTok  
**Music:** Instrumental, ambient, 100–120 BPM (royalty-free: Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or similar)  
**Voiceover:** Calm, conversational. No rush.

---

## Visual Storyboard + Script

**[0:00–1s] COLD OPEN**
- **Visual:** Black screen, white text: "3 questions."
- **VO:** "3 questions."
- **Music:** Fade in, ambient swell

**[1–3s] THE QUESTION**
- **Visual:** App opens. Sanctuary Profiler appears. Single question onscreen: "Where are you when the world gets loud?"
- **VO:** "One question reveals your fragrance DNA."
- **Tap:** Swipe to first persona

**[3–5s] THE REVEAL (PERSONA 1)**
- **Visual:** Card slides in. Large text: "VELVET INTELLECTUAL"
- **Subtext:** "Moody. Introspective. Niche-loving."
- **VO:** "Velvet Intellectual. Moody. Introspective. Niche."
- **Tap:** Swipe to wardrobe

**[5–7s] THE WARDROBE**
- **Visual:** Grid of 4–6 bottle cards appears. Beautiful imagery, gradient overlays, family colors.
- **Bottles visible:** e.g., Parfums de Nicolaï, L'Eau Pénétrante, raw oud.
- **VO:** "Your wardrobe. Curated for you."
- **Tap:** Swipe to show dupe chain

**[7–9s] THE DUPE REVEAL**
- **Visual:** Tap on one bottle. Dupe chain expands: "Your £150 → £18 alternative."
- **Show prices side-by-side:** £150 Creed vs. £18 Lattafa.
- **VO:** "Every bottle has a dupe. Same scent. Different price."
- **CTA text overlay:** "Free. No account. No algorithm."

**[9–11s] PERSONA SWAP (QUICK MONTAGE)**
- **Visual:** Swipe left. New persona card appears: "SOLAR MINIMALIST"
- **Swipe left again.** "DARK ALCHEMIST"
- **Show 2–3 bottles per persona, quick cuts.**
- **VO:** "Pick your personality. See your wardrobe."
- **Music:** Builds slightly

**[11–13s] CTA + BRANDING**
- **Visual:** App logo. Text: "SCENTRAL" in large letters.
- **URL underlay:** "scentral-seven.vercel.app"
- **VO:** "Scentral. Find your fragrance DNA."
- **Music:** Peaks, holds

**[13–15s] CLOSING SHOT**
- **Visual:** App icon bounces gently. Tagline: "Free. No account. No algorithm."
- **VO:** "Available now on iOS and Android."
- **CTA button:** "Download" or app store badges
- **Music:** Fade to ambient

## Production Notes

### Camera / Recording
- **Device:** iPhone or iPad in portrait mode (9:16)
- **Resolution:** 1080p minimum (1440p if you have it)
- **Framerate:** 30fps (24fps if cinematic feel desired)
- **Lighting:** Natural daylight or ring light (bright, shadowless)
- **Stabilization:** Tripod or gimbal (no handheld jitter)

### Screen Recording
- **Use:** iOS Screen Recorder (Settings > Control Center > Screen Recording) or Android equivalent
- **Background:** Solid dark background if not showing full device frame (optional)
- **Speed:** Normal speed (1x). No slow-mo until the final reveal shot if you want drama.

### Editing
- **Software:** Final Cut Pro, Adobe Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, or CapCut (free)
- **Timeline:** 15s exactly (no padding)
- **Transitions:** Crossfades or swipe (2–3 frames, 200ms max—keep it snappy)
- **Text:** White sans-serif (no shadows, high contrast). Use Unbounded or Instrument Serif (brand fonts).
- **Color grading:** Slight warm shift (Scentral brand feel—amber/gold tint, not oversaturated)

### Audio
- **Voiceover:** Recorded separately in quiet room
  - Speak slowly, clearly, no emotion (conversational, not hyped)
  - Record at -3dB to -6dB peak (headroom for music)
  - Export as WAV or AIFF
- **Music track:** 15s clip (intro at 0:00, peaks by 12s, tail by 15s)
  - Source: Epidemic Sound, Artlist, YouTube Audio Library, or Soundcloud (royalty-free)
  - License: Ensure "YouTube" and "commercial use" rights
  - BPM: 100–120 (calm, not frantic)
  - Genre: Ambient, lo-fi, or indie electronic
- **Mix:** VO at -12dB, music at -9dB during VO, -6dB on peaks (no clipping)

### Final Export
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Bitrate:** 5–8 Mbps (YouTube-ready quality)
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 (portrait)
- **Filename:** `scentral-demo-15s-final.mp4`

## Usage Across Platforms

### Twitter / X
- Upload directly as video tweet (max 2.2 GB, 15 min max—you're well under)
- Tweet copy: "Here's Scentral in 15 seconds. 3 questions. One persona. 280 fragrances. No BS. scentral-seven.vercel.app"

### Instagram Reels
- Exact 9:16 format (no cropping needed)
- Add captions: "Your fragrance DNA in 15 seconds"
- Link in bio

### TikTok
- Upload as-is (9:16, auto-fit)
- Caption: "POV: You're finding your scent in 15 seconds #fragrance #app #scentral"
- Use TikTok's trending audio OR upload your own voiceover track

### Product Hunt
- Upload as embedded video in PH post
- Show in Hero section or as a gallery item

### Facebook / LinkedIn
- Square or landscape crop (1:1 or 16:9) for feed
- OR full 9:16 vertical (will display as vertical video in feed)

## A/B Testing (Optional)
Film TWO versions if you have time:

**Version A:** Persona reveal (current script)
- Hook: "Find YOUR fragrance personality"
- Audience: Fragrance collectors, personality enthusiasts

**Version B:** Dupe reveal (alternate focus)
- Hook: "Save £100+ on every bottle"
- Audience: Budget-conscious, value-hunters
- Script change: Lead with dupe, end with persona

Post both to Twitter. See which gets more engagement. Use winner for other platforms.

## Backup: If You Don't Have Time to Film
You can use screen recordings + text overlays (lower production value, but still works):
- Faster to produce (2–3 hours vs. half a day)
- Still conveys the concept
- Use CapCut (free, mobile-friendly editor) to add text + music
- Downsides: less cinematic, might not stop the scroll on TikTok

**Decision:** If launch is within 48 hours, go with the screen-recording backup. If you have a week, invest 4–6 hours in the full production version.
