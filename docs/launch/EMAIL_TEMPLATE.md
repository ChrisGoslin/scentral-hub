## Segmentation Notes
- **If you have email data:** segment by signup reason (fragrance interest vs vague "early access")
- **Optional personalization:** if user mentioned a fragrance in signup, call it out: "You asked about Creed Royal Oud. We found your dupe (and 5 others)."

## Follow-up Cadence (T+3, T+7, T+14)
- **T+3:** "Here's what early users found" (social proof email with download count + persona distribution)
- **T+7:** "Don't sleep on the dupes" (feature highlight email with example dupe chains)
- **T+14:** "Your wardrobe is waiting" (last re-engagement email before marketing pause)

Use same tone: direct, personal, no fluff.
