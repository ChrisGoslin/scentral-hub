### App Name (29 chars)

### Subtitle (29 chars)

### Description (2,421 / 4000 chars)

That €140 bottle you've worn for years? nota. just found its €18 twin.

We call it an inspired-by match, and it's usually the first thing people discover when they open the app. Turns out the fragrance world is full of these — you just need a place that tells you.

nota. is the easy way to build, track, and actually understand your fragrance collection. No forums, no gatekeeping, no five-star judgment. Just your bottles, your notes, and a community that believes every nose is different — including yours.

**Find out what you're really wearing**
Search a catalogue of hundreds of fragrances and see, instantly, which designer and niche scents have affordable inspired-by alternatives. No more wondering if you're overpaying.

**Build your wardrobe, not a spreadsheet**
Add the bottles you own, are testing, or have decanted, and watch them settle onto your shelf — organised automatically into Top Signatures, Occasion Modifiers, Base Anchors, and a Holding Zone for the ones you haven't bonded with yet. A small badge always shows you what's a full bottle, a decant, a tester, or still on your wishlist.

**Log how it actually wears on you**
Skip the star ratings. nota.'s Wear Log walks you through three honest checkpoints — First Spray, The Heart, and the Dry Down — so you build a real record of how a scent evolves on your skin, not just a snap judgment.

**Find the right bottle in three taps**
Sensory Lenses are one-tap shortcuts for real life: Agadir Nights for hot evenings out, Executive for the office, Comfort for the days you just want your safety scent. No advanced knowledge required.

**Discover your scent identity**
A short, plain-language quiz reveals your scent persona — Velvet Intellectual, Solar Minimalist, or Dark Alchemist — and quietly shapes what nota. shows you next.

**Go further when you're ready**
Layer fragrances in the Lab, browse curated fragrance content in Social, find your scent twins with DNA Match, and set up a daily Spritz Schedule. nota.'s core wardrobe tools are free forever — Pro unlocks deeper wardrobe intelligence when you want it.

nota. was built on one rule: "Yes, and." Whatever you smell, however you describe it, however small or strange your collection — your fingerprint is valid. There's no wrong way to love fragrance here.

Start with the bottles you already own. You'll probably learn something about them in the first five minutes.

## --- ASO NOTES ---

### Competitor positioning (verified via App Store search, June 2026)

1. **Fragrantica** — the category's 800-lb gorilla: massive database, deep community reviews, note breakdowns. But it's built for enthusiasts who already speak the language — forum tone, jargon-heavy, easy for a newcomer to feel like they walked into the wrong room. nota.'s entire pitch is the opposite: plain language, "Yes, and," zero gatekeeping.
2. **Parfumo** — strong at collection tracking with stats and new-release alerts, but same enthusiast-first framing as Fragrantica and similarly community/forum-flavored. Doesn't have anything like an inspired-by dupe-match surfaced at the point of discovery.
3. **Scentbird** — primarily a sample subscription box with light collection tools bolted on. It's selling you new bottles, not helping you understand or organise what you already own. nota. has no subscription-box motive — it's wardrobe-first.

### Keyword gaps nota. can own

1. **"dupe" / "clone" / "smells like"** — none of the three above lead with dupe-finding as a core, front-and-center feature. Fragrantica/Parfumo communities discuss dupes informally in forum threads, but no app surfaces an inspired-by match at the moment you're browsing a fragrance card. nota.'s `inspired_by` data does this natively — this is the single best owned keyword cluster.
2. **"beginner" / "newcomer" fragrance app** — all three competitors skew toward people who already collect seriously and speak the vocabulary (accords, sillage, batch codes). "Fragrance app for beginners" is underserved; nota.'s plain-language Sensory Lenses and persona quiz are built exactly for that search intent.
