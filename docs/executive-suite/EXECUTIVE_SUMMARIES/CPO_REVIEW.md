# Executive Review: Chief Product Officer

## 🧬 Product Roadmap & Feature Alignment

Scentral Hub currently delivers 100% on its core promise: Curate, Synthesize, Plan. The nomenclature shift to "The Wardrobe" and "The Atelier" has fundamentally changed the user's emotional connection to the features.

### Feature Evaluation:
- **The Wardrobe:** 10/10. Visuals are stunning and the metadata is accurate.
- **The Atelier:** 9/10. Synthesis logic is powerful, but needs a "Save to Favorites" quick-action for recurring formulations.
- **The Ritual:** 8/10. Functionally complete, but needs push notification support (PWA) to remind users of their evening spritz.

### The Next Frontier:
- **The Community:** We need to move beyond "The You." The product's true scale lies in users discovering resonance patterns from *other* ateliers. 

**Verdict:** 95% Feature-Complete for Demo. Roadmap is clear and aligned with the "Sensory Sovereignty" vision.
