# BaseNote Launch Marketing — Template & Copy Direction

**Campaign Period:** 2 weeks before → 4 weeks after app store launch  
**Audience:** Fragrance enthusiasts, journal/habit trackers, indie beauty culture  
**Budget Allocation (if any):** 60% social organic + earned media, 30% email, 10% paid social tests

## Marketing Strategy Foundation (1000+ Prompts Persona Mapping)

### Business Profile
- **Organization:** nota. (BaseNote)
- **Product:** Personal fragrance identity & collection platform
- **USP:** Track what *moves you*, not what *impresses others*. Anti-influencer, pro-intention
- **Category Challenge:** Fragrance discovery is dominated by luxury marketing (Scentbird, Fragbuy, unbox subscriptions)
- **BaseNote Angle:** Journal + mirror (not shopping cart)

### Target Persona
- **Name:** Aria (28, urban, independent; or Sam, 32, suburban, curious)
- **Psychographics:**
  - Distrusts influencer beauty culture
  - Values personal identity over trend-following
  - Enjoys rituals and intentional living
  - Willing to spend on quality but skeptical of "premium" branding
  - Active on: TikTok (for culture, not purchasing), Instagram (aesthetic, not ads), Reddit (r/fragrance), Indie podcast communities
  - Reads: Kinfolk, Substack indie culture newsletters, Medium essays on identity
- **Pain Points:**
  - Too many fragrances; hard to remember which ones I love
  - Fragrance recs are biased (influencers paid, friends' taste ≠ mine)
  - Want to track mood + scent over time (ritual awareness)
  - Embarrassed to pay $150+ for a fragrance I'll forget about in 3 months
- **Aspirations:**
  - Build a meaningful fragrance collection (not a closet)
  - Understand *why* I'm drawn to certain scents
  - Use fragrance as a tool for mood/identity intentionality
  - Share scent stories with friends (without it feeling like hawking)

## Metrics & Success Criteria

### Launch Week Goals
- **Downloads:** 100+ (aggressive goal: 250+)
- **Waitlist → Install conversion:** 30%+
- **D1 Retention:** 20%+ (return within 24 hours)
- **Email open rate:** 35%+
- **Social media reach:** 5k–10k impressions on launch announcement posts

### Month 1 Goals
- **Downloads:** 500+ cumulative
- **DAU:** 50–100
- **Feature adoption:** >70% try Shelf, >40% try Blind Ranking, >20% log Traces
- **Average session length:** 5+ minutes
- **D7 Retention:** 5%+
- **Organic referrals:** 10%+ of new users

### Milestone Markers
- **Week 2:** Launch Spritz Schedule feature (increase re-engagement)
- **Week 3:** Ship first "How to Get Started" video series (YouTube Shorts)
- **Week 4:** Highlight top user stories (media + social)
- **Month 2:** Plan Phase 8 features (social sharing, gifting)

---

## Contingency: Low Launch Response

If first week is slow (<50 installs):
1. **Double down on TikTok** — Organic reach is free; ship 2–3 viral-ready videos
2. **Reactivate email list** — Resend to open-but-non-converters with social proof
3. **Fragrance community outreach** — Reddit AMAs, indie beauty Slack groups, podcast guests
4. **Press follow-up** — HubSpot/email pitch to tech/lifestyle journalists (missed initial press?)
5. **User interviews** — Talk to 5–10 waitlist no-shows; understand barriers
