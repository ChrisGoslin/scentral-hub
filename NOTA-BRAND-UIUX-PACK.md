# nota. — UI/UX Brand & Design Pack

## 0. THE DIRECTION CHANGED 
**Retired positioning:** "quiet luxury," "Scentral," "BaseNote." Do not design for it. 
**Current positioning:** The modern, authentic fragrance chemist's workshop. A perfumer's lab notebook × Aesop's material honesty × Apple Notes' calm utility. 
**Banned:** Perfume bottles as glamour, gold, black marble, generic SaaS, crypto/Web3 visuals.

## 1. Typography — 90 / 10
- **The Instrument — Geist (~90%):** Navigation, body, labels, chemistry. Uppercase labels track `+0.15em`.
- **The Vessel — Instrument Serif Italic (~10%):** Identity names, reveals, memories. Tracking `-0.01em`. This is wet ink.

## 2. Materials — The Workshop
- **Paper:** Ivory base, subtle 2% grain (one fixed compositor layer). 
- **Ink & charcoal:** `mix-blend-mode: multiply` so ink sinks into paper.
- **Lab evidence:** Hand-labelled vials, masking tape, ink annotations, botanical/charcoal studies.
- **Real assets only:** Scanned with genuine ink bleed. Never code-drawn SVG approximations of nature.
- **Liquid glass:** Floating nav/overlays only. Max 3 glass layers per viewport.

## 3. Experience Doctrine
Recognition before interaction. Expression before analytics. Understanding before commerce. 
**War on cognitive load:** One decision per screen; ≤3 actions per screen; no dead ends. 
**Commerce (Temptations):** Rare, subtle, never urgency. Always offers "maybe later". 

## 4. Surface Glossary
The Read (identity reveal) · Noseprint (behavioural scent identity) · Shelf (what you actually wear) · Blind Ranking (bias removal) · Traces (expression) · Trails (guided understanding paths) · Insights (reflection) · Today's Arc (time-aware dashboard).

## 5. Anti-Slop Checklist
Reject: Generic white cards on colour, rounded rectangles everywhere, arbitrary glassmorphism, badge gamification, centred symmetry by default.
