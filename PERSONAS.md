# Scentral Hub: Customer Personas

## Persona 1: **Luna** — The Niche Collector
**Age:** 28–40 | **Location:** Middle East, Europe | **Annual Spend:** $2,000–5,000+

### Profile
Luna is obsessed with fragrance as *art*. She owns 20+ bottles, follows fragrance influencers obsessively, visits boutiques monthly, and can describe top/heart/base notes with precision. Luxury is non-negotiable; she refuses mass-market fragrances. Oud, natural extracts, and vintage releases drive her purchases.

### Goals
- Discover rare, exclusive fragrances before they sell out
- Curate a "signature collection" that reflects her identity
- Share her taste with like-minded collectors
- Understand fragrance chemistry deeply (notes, accords, combinations)

### Behaviors
- Subscribes to fragrance blogs and YouTube reviews
- Attends fragrance events and private tastings
- Saves collections, creates wishlists, shares recommendations
- Willing to pay premium for exclusivity and education

### Frustrations
- Fragrances sell out; no reliable way to access rare scents
- Hard to find detailed note breakdowns; reviews are shallow
- No platform to showcase her collection or trade with other collectors
- Fragrance discovery feels random without curation

### What She Values
✅ **Exclusivity** — Early access to limited editions  
✅ **Education** — Deep fragrance knowledge, expert insights  
✅ **Community** — Connect with other enthusiasts  
✅ **Curation** — Personalized recommendations based on taste  

### What She Avoids
❌ Mass-market fragrances (Sephora bestsellers feel generic)  
❌ Overly commercial platforms (too much advertising)  
❌ Fragrance dupes (she wants authenticity)  
❌ Subscription boxes (unpredictable selections)  

### Premium Features She'd Pay For
- **Exclusive access** — Pre-order rare fragrances before general release
- **Personalized blending** — Custom combos blended by experts
- **Fragrance ratings** — Expert community reviews from verified buyers
- **Curated collections** — Seasonal/themed recommendations from specialists
- **VIP tastings** — Access to private fragrance events

## Persona 2: **Marcus** — The Confident Buyer
**Age:** 35–50 | **Location:** US, UK | **Annual Spend:** $500–1,500

### Profile
Marcus has a few "go-to" fragrances he's worn for years. He's not obsessed, but he values quality and wants a scent that projects confidence. He buys gifts for partners and colleagues. He's skeptical of hype but respects expertise. He researches before buying but trusts recommendations from trusted sources (friends, bartenders, barbers).

### Goals
- Find 2–3 signature scents he can rotate confidently
- Look put-together without overthinking fragrance
- Give thoughtful fragrance gifts that land well
- Understand fragrance basics (what makes a scent work on him)

### Behaviors
- Reads reviews before buying; asks friends for advice
- Buys from department stores or niche boutiques
- Usually buys the same fragrance; tries new ones sparingly
- Rarely layers or combines fragrances

### Frustrations
- Fragrance selection is overwhelming; too many options
- Hard to know if a fragrance will work on *his* skin chemistry
- Limited sampling options online
- Doesn't understand fragrance note language; feels excluded

### What He Values
✅ **Simplicity** — Clear, actionable recommendations  
✅ **Confidence** — Knowing a scent is right before buying  
✅ **Quality** — Fragrances that last, perform well  
✅ **Social proof** — Trusted reviews and recommendations  

### What He Avoids
❌ Analysis paralysis (too many choices)  
❌ Fragrance snobs (gatekeeping language)  
❌ Weak or fleeting scents  
❌ Overly feminine or juvenile fragrances  

### Premium Features He'd Pay For
- **Scent matching** — "Find fragrances for your skin chemistry"
- **Sampling service** — Receive 3–5 samples targeted to his taste
- **Expert guidance** — 1-on-1 consultation with fragrance expert
- **Loyalty rewards** — Discounts, exclusive releases, birthday gifts
- **Gift recommendations** — AI-powered gift suggestions for friends

## Persona 3: **Zara** — The Gifter & Casual Explorer
**Age:** 22–35 | **Location:** Global (TikTok-native) | **Annual Spend:** $200–600

### Profile
Zara discovers fragrances through TikTok and Instagram. She buys perfume for herself and others, but it's an occasional, trend-driven purchase. She likes fragrances that smell *good* (fruity, sweet, fresh) but doesn't care about notes or longevity. She's influenced by creator endorsements and aesthetics. Price-sensitive; balks at $100+ bottles but splurges on trending scents.

### Goals
- Find affordable fragrances that smell amazing
- Discover trending scents before friends
- Get compliments on her fragrance choices
- Find the perfect gift fragrance (hassle-free)

### Behaviors
- Discovers fragrances through TikTok/Instagram creators
- Buys from Sephora, Urban Outfitters, Amazon
- Trades recommendations with friends via text/chat
- Watches unboxing and fragrance hauls

### Frustrations
- Fragrance terminology (top/heart/base) feels pretentious
- Hard to know if a trending scent will work for her
- No easy way to share favorites with friends
- Limited sampling options; don't want to commit to full bottle

### What She Values
✅ **Accessibility** — Affordable, easy to find  
✅ **Trendiness** — What's hot right now  
✅ **Aesthetics** — Pretty bottles, nice unboxing  
✅ **Social proof** — Creator endorsements  

### What She Avoids
❌ Pretentious fragrance language  
❌ Expensive, "adult" fragrances  
❌ Niche or unfamiliar brands  
❌ Fragrances that don't smell "nice" immediately  

### Premium Features She'd Pay For
- **Fragrance samples** — Cheap sample packs ($9.99–19.99)
- **Creator collections** — Exclusive fragrance collab with TikTok creators
- **Trending tracker** — What fragrances are viral right now
- **Share & earn** — Invite friends, get discounts
- **Fragrance quizzes** — Fun filters to find "your scent"
