# Scentral Hub: User Research Plan

## Objectives

1. **Define customer persona(s)** — Who buys/explores fragrances? Age, lifestyle, spending, values.
2. **Understand purchase driver** — What triggers fragrance discovery? Occasion? Recommendation? Experimentation?
3. **Map pain points** — Where do customers get stuck? Selection paralysis? Price anxiety? Sampling access?
4. **Identify feature priorities** — What would make the Layering Lab valuable? Sharing combos? Saving favorites? Fragrance education?
5. **Validate Premium tier appetite** — Would customers pay for advanced features (custom blends, personalized recommendations, exclusive fragrances)?

---

## Research Methods

### Primary: User Interviews (5-8 participants)
- **Focus:** Deep dive on fragrance behavior, values, and frustrations
- **Duration:** 45–60 min per interview
- **Format:** 1-on-1, qualitative
- **Sample:** Mix of:
  - Luxury fragrance buyers (frequent, high-spend)
  - Casual explorers (occasional, gift-driven)
  - Niche enthusiasts (oud, natural, vintage focused)
  - Geographic diversity (US, Middle East, Europe)

### Secondary: Usability Testing (Layering Lab prototype)
- **Focus:** Watch users interact with the combiner; observe confusion, delight, hesitation
- **Duration:** 30–45 min per session
- **Format:** Remote screen share, 5–6 participants
- **Task:** "Explore fragrance combinations; tell me what works, what's confusing"

### Tertiary: Surveys (50–100 responses)
- **Focus:** Quantify attitudes: price sensitivity, feature preferences, channel preferences
- **Format:** Online form (Typeform, Google Forms)
- **Sample:** Fragrance enthusiasts from Reddit (r/fragrance), TikTok, Instagram

---

## Interview Guide

### Warm-up (5 min)
- Thanks for joining
- This session is about understanding how you explore and buy fragrances
- No right/wrong answers; your honest feedback shapes our product

### Context: Current Behavior (10 min)
- How often do you buy fragrances? What's your typical spend per year?
- Walk me through your last fragrance purchase. What triggered it?
- Do you prefer single scents or do you layer/combine fragrances? Why?

### Deep Dive: Fragrance Journey (20 min)
- How do you discover new fragrances? (Influencers, friends, boutiques, online reviews?)
- What makes you *confident* a fragrance is right for you before buying?
- Tell me about a fragrance that disappointed you. What went wrong?
- Do you have a "signature" scent, or do you like variety?
- What's your relationship with fragrance notes (top/heart/base)? Do you use that language?

### Reaction to Layering Concept (10 min)
- If you could combine 2-3 fragrances into a custom blend, would that appeal to you?
- Would you want to save your combinations? Share them with friends?
- What would make a layering tool irresistible?

### Premium Features (10 min)
- Would you pay for personalized fragrance recommendations?
- Would you pay to access exclusive or rare fragrances?
- What's a "premium" feature you'd happily pay for in a fragrance app?

### Wrap-up (5 min)
- Anything we didn't ask about?
- Can we follow up if we have questions?

## Usability Testing Script

**Pre-test (5 min):**
- "We're testing a fragrance combining tool. We want to see how you naturally use it."
- "Think aloud: tell me what you're clicking, why you're doing it, what confuses you."

**Task (25–30 min):**
1. "Explore this tool. Pick any 2 fragrances and see what combo you get."
2. "Try adding a third. What changed?"
3. "Remove one. What do you notice about the score?"
4. "If you loved this combo, how would you save it? Share it?"
5. "What would make this tool more useful for you?"

**Debrief (5 min):**
- "What worked well?"
- "What confused you?"
- "Would you use this again?"

## Survey Topics

- Fragrance purchase frequency & spend
- Primary discovery channel (influencer, friend, store, online)
- Feature interest ranking (layering, recommendations, rare access, saved collections, social sharing)
- Price sensitivity (would you pay $9.99/mo, $19.99/mo, $29.99/mo for premium?)
- Preferred fragrance profile (fresh, floral, woody, spicy, niche, designer)
- Geography & cultural preferences

## Success Metrics

✅ **Feature validation:** Top 3 most-requested features identified  
✅ **Premium pricing:** Know acceptable price point & feature set  
✅ **Launch prioritization:** Clear roadmap for next 6 months
