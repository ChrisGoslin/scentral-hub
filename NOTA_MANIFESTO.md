## 1. MARKET POSITION

nota. occupies the empty quadrant of high utility and deep subjectivity. We reject the sterile encyclopedic mechanics of Fragrantica and the glossy transaction-heavy storefronts of Sephora. The territory we claim is the authentic working fragrance chemist's workshop: kinetic execution, tactile material physics, and an environment where the architecture listens and reflects rather than shouts and sells.

## 2. THE CURATOR (Consumer Psychology)
We serve **Curators** and **Contributors**. Fragrance is a self-authored identity signal.

*   **Identity Construction:** They assemble a wardrobe to dictate who they are. They seek recognition.
*   **Fear of Incorrect Execution:** We build trust through radical honesty and transparent chemistry.
*   **Motivational Interviewing (MI) Stance:** We reflect and explore. "One person's trash is another person's treasure." Your nose is your absolute truth.

We build specifically for two practical archetypes:

*   **Luna, the Collector:** identity-led, emotionally literate, building a scent wardrobe that feels like authorship rather than consumption.
*   **Marcus, the Confident Buyer:** practical, decisive, and allergic to wasted spend, blind-buy regret, and recommendation theater.

Neither wants algorithmic commands or generic gamification. They want recognition, belonging without gatekeeping, and radically candid observations they can trust.

## 3. KINETIC APPLICATION & EXPLAINABLE CHEMISTRY (The 20% Push)
We do not just track bottles; we manipulate physical reality.

*   **Kinetic Application Mechanics:** The UI must guide the physical application of the molecule. We instruct on:
    1. *Lipid Primer Superimposition:* Non-polar substrates to trap volatile notes.
    2. *Spatial Cloud Dispersion:* Micro-droplet saturation for sillage.
    3. *Textile Fixation:* Fabric application for linear aquatic profiles.
*   **XAI (Explainable AI) Transparency:** No black boxes. When the system recommends an accord, it must offer a quiet, accessible data lineage explaining *why* (e.g., shared heart notes, opposing molecular weights).
*   **Evaporation UI (Live Activity):** Fragrance is a clock that runs on skin. UI components tracking a current wear must physically decay over time, visually shifting from Top (100%) to Heart (50%) to Base (10%) notes.
